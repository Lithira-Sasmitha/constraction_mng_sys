OUR PROJECTS

Malapalla Housing Complex.


  • Client : Ministry of Transport.

  • Completion Date : 2024 (planned).

  • Location : Maharagama, Colombo District.


The project is a component of the government’s Railway Efficiency Improvement Project (REIP) and Colombo Suburban Railway Project (CSRP) implemented by the Ministry of Transport and funded by the Asian Development Bank (ADB). The project involves construction of a medium-rise 10-storey housing complex at Malapalla with 120 apartments and necessary amenities, covering a total floor area of around 125,000 square feet. The complex will accommodate families living in informal settlements along the railway corridor, who will need to relocate in order to complete expansion and improvement of the Kelani Valley Railway Line, which passes through the Maharagama and Homagama DS Divisions.
