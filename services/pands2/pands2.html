SanMech (Pvt) Ltd is an associated company of SL2MY Construction (Pvt) Ltd - a leading construction company in Sri Lanka and overseas. Sanmech (Pvt) Ltd is the management arm of the heavy and light machinery fleet of SL2MY Construction (Pvt) Ltd which comprises the best machinery fleet in Sri Lanka. The fleet is capable of handling construction work of any magnitude.


Apart from SL2MY Construction (Pvt) Ltd, we have provided our machinery a wide range of other construction companies which are among the best in the field of construction in Sri Lanka.We strive to provide the best equipmentpossible and we are proud to say that we have the equipment to cover all your construction needs.


SanMech (Pvt) Ltd offers a diverse fleet of machinery which includes cranes, concrete pumps, loaders, trucks, generators, air compressors, hoists, batching plants, and many more. SanMech is constantly upgrading its machinery fleet to be the best, latest machinery fleet in Sri Lanka. SanMech has strong environmental concerns too. It always gives the utmost priority to keep the fleet to comply with the government’s emission regulations.
